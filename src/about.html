Felucca is an open firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, a small synthesizer built around a JieLi pi32v2 DSP. Its sound engines are plain integer C, self-contained, and each one is a different way of 'whistling'.

Fluyt takes nine of them and ships each as its own KORG drumlogue or KORG microKORG2 unit. Please visit Fluyt on RGRV.nl to download the Logue units for device.
